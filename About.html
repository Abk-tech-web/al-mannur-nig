Who We Are

Al-Mannur Nigeria Enterprises is a trusted name in the foodstuff and provisions business. Based in Sabon Gari Main Market, Zaria, Kaduna, we are committed to providing top-quality food items at competitive prices. Our product range includes essential food items such as Flour, Sugar, Spaghetti, Macaroni, Indomie, Cooking Oil and much more. We serve both retail and wholesale customers, ensuring satisfaction through genuine and quality supply.

Our Vision

To be the leading provider of foodstuff and provision products in Nigeria, maintaining excellence, affordability, and a reputation built on trust.

Our Mission

To supply households and businesses with quality food products, delivered with care, honesty, and a commitment to customer satisfaction.
